Instrument

ZEISS LSM 880

Confocal laser scanning microscope with Airyscan detection

A high-performance confocal imaging platform for high-resolution fluorescence imaging, optical sectioning, three-dimensional imaging, and advanced cellular microscopy.

Confocal Airyscan Z-Stack 3D Imaging
Technology

Imaging Capabilities

The LSM 880 combines laser scanning confocal microscopy with advanced Airyscan detection, providing high-resolution optical sectioning and flexible fluorescence imaging for biological research.

Confocal Imaging

High-resolution optical sectioning for fluorescence imaging of cells, tissues, and biological structures.

Airyscan Detection

Airyscan detection provides improved resolution and signal-to-noise compared with conventional confocal detection, enabling detailed imaging of fine cellular structures.

Z-Stack & 3D Imaging

Acquire optical sections through biological specimens for three-dimensional visualization and analysis of cellular structures.

Multichannel Fluorescence

Acquire multiple fluorescence channels for localization studies, colocalization, and comparative analysis of biological structures.

Advanced Imaging

Airyscan Imaging

ZEISS LSM 880 with Airyscan detection.

High-resolution imaging with improved signal-to-noise

Airyscan detection uses a detector array to collect spatially resolved fluorescence information, enabling improved resolution and signal-to-noise while retaining the optical sectioning capabilities of confocal microscopy.

This makes the system particularly useful for resolving fine cellular structures, weak fluorescent signals, and detailed subcellular organization.

Applications

What Can Be Imaged?

Cellular Organization

Visualization of cellular structures and subcellular organization using fluorescence and optical sectioning.

Protein Localization

Fluorescence imaging for investigating protein distribution and localization within cells and tissues.

Organelle Imaging

High-resolution imaging of organelles and fine intracellular structures.

3D Cellular Structures

Z-stack acquisition for three-dimensional visualization of cells, tissues, and multicellular structures.

Plant–Microbe Interactions

Fluorescence imaging of interactions between plant cells, microorganisms, and their associated structures.

Live-Cell Imaging

Time-resolved fluorescence imaging for observing cellular processes and dynamic biological behavior.
