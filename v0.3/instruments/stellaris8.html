Leica STELLARIS 8

White-light laser confocal platform

A flexible confocal imaging platform for high-resolution fluorescence imaging, spectral detection, live-cell imaging, and advanced quantitative microscopy.

Confocal Spectral FLIM FRAP
Technology

Imaging Capabilities

The STELLARIS 8 combines flexible spectral detection with advanced fluorescence imaging capabilities, supporting a wide range of biological applications.

Confocal Imaging

High-resolution optical sectioning for fluorescence imaging of cells, tissues, and biological structures.

Spectral Imaging

Spectral detection for separating fluorescence signals and challenging multi-label experiments.

FLIM

Fluorescence lifetime imaging for quantitative measurements of fluorescent probes and molecular interactions.

FRAP

Fluorescence recovery after photobleaching for studying molecular mobility and dynamics.
