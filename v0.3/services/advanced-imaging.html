Advanced Fluorescence

Beyond conventional fluorescence imaging

Advanced imaging techniques can provide information beyond conventional fluorescence intensity, including improved spatial resolution, spectral separation, fluorescence lifetime and molecular dynamics.

The appropriate approach depends on your biological question, sample characteristics and the type of information required from the experiment.

Not sure which advanced imaging approach is appropriate for your experiment? We can help evaluate your sample and identify a suitable imaging strategy.

Advanced Imaging Capabilities

✦

Airyscan Imaging

Enhanced spatial resolution and signal-to-noise for high-resolution fluorescence imaging.

Spectral Imaging

Spectral detection and separation of overlapping fluorescence signals for multiplex imaging.

◌

FLIM

Fluorescence lifetime imaging for quantitative measurements of molecular and cellular environments.

⇄

FRET

Fluorescence resonance energy transfer imaging for studying molecular interactions and proximity.

↔

FRAP

Fluorescence recovery after photobleaching for analyzing molecular mobility and dynamics.

Quantitative Imaging

Generate image datasets suitable for quantitative measurements, downstream analysis and biological interpretation.

Applications

What can we investigate?

Advanced imaging approaches can be used when conventional fluorescence imaging does not provide enough spatial, spectral or functional information.

  • High-resolution cellular structures
  • Spectral separation of fluorescent signals
  • Fluorescence lifetime changes
  • Protein–protein interactions
  • Molecular proximity and FRET
  • Protein and membrane dynamics
  • Molecular mobility and FRAP recovery
