Dynamic Biology

From Static Images to Biological Dynamics

Live-cell imaging allows researchers to observe biological systems as they change over time. By combining fluorescence microscopy, time-lapse acquisition, and environmental control, dynamic processes can be captured and quantified in living cells and tissues.

What We Offer

Live-Cell Imaging Capabilities

Time-Lapse Imaging

Capture biological processes continuously over minutes, hours, or longer time periods to reveal cellular dynamics.

Environmental Control

Maintain controlled imaging conditions for experiments requiring stable temperature, atmosphere, or extended observation.

Long-Term Imaging

Monitor cellular and tissue behavior over extended periods while minimizing phototoxicity and maintaining image quality.

Cellular Dynamics

Visualize processes such as cell division, migration, organelle dynamics, and changes in cellular morphology.

Fluorescence Tracking

Follow fluorescently labeled structures and molecules through time to characterize movement, localization, and dynamics.

Quantitative Dynamics

Combine time-resolved imaging with image analysis to measure movement, growth, intensity changes, and cellular behavior.

From Image to Dynamics

Follow Biological Processes Through Time

Live-cell imaging provides more than individual snapshots. Time-resolved acquisition can reveal when and where biological events occur and how cellular systems respond to changing conditions.

  • Cell division and proliferation
  • Cell migration and morphology
  • Organelle dynamics
  • Fluorescence intensity changes
  • Long-term cellular responses
