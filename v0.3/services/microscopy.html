Applications

What can we visualize?

Microscopy can be used to investigate structures and processes across plant, microbial and cellular systems.

  • Cellular organization
  • Protein localization
  • Organelle morphology
  • Fluorescence colocalization
  • Tissue and multicellular structures
  • Cell morphology and dynamics
  • Plant–microbe interactions
